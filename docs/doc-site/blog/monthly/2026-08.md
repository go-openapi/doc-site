August was carried by a single repository: **codescan** accounted for close to half of the month's roughly 400 load-bearing commits across the two organisations. The work there was a broad overhaul — a reworked dependency **loader** that can read dependencies from source or from compiled packages, a hardened `genspec` terminal UI, and a run of scanner fixes — culminating in codescan v0.36.4. The other thread worth noticing was a coordinated correctness wave through the spec pipeline: fixes started in `spec` and `analysis` and were onboarded downstream into `loads`, `validate` and finally `go-swagger`, so several repos' commit logs this month are the same story seen from different layers.

## Themes

- **codescan overhaul.** A new configurable loader (source vs. compiled dependencies, exposed through a `-loader` flag and CI matrix), scanner correctness fixes (`swagger:meta` placement, standard-library and vendored-import resolution, go1.27 `jsontext` alias handling), a hardened `genspec` TUI, and reworked benchmarks.
- **Spec-pipeline correctness.** `spec` aligned `$ref` and base-URL normalization with `jsonreference` and made schema expansion deterministic (sorted map walks, gob round-trip fixes); `analysis` fixed flattening of `$ref`s held under unmapped keywords; `validate` stopped rewriting the caller's document during validation and re-instated its error-message stability tests. Each was onboarded downstream.
- **testify maintenance.** A batch of assertion correctness fixes (mixed-sign `EqualValues`, `InDeltaSlice`/`InDeltaMapValues` reporting, rune/byte `Contains`), a new `ErrorNotContains` assertion, and generic forward methods — released as v2.7.0, alongside a published roadmap.
- **go-swagger generator.** Numerous generator fixes and new CLI flags (`--with-go-run`, `--no-default-omit-empty`, `--generate-getters`), discriminator/`allOf` model handling, nil-pointer guards, and generated markdown CLI usage docs.
- **Security & CI hardening.** Control-character/escape handling in the codescan TUI, template-injection autofixes and refreshed change-detection in ci-workflows, and a Docker base-image patch in go-swagger.
